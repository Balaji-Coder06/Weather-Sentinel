# 🌦️ Weather Sentinel

**Context-Aware Environmental Risk Intelligence**

Weather Sentinel goes beyond traditional weather apps by turning forecasts into **actionable outdoor activity decisions**.

Instead of simply showing temperature, rain, and wind, it considers your **activity, location, duration, time, and environmental conditions** to estimate contextual risk and recommend better activity windows.

## ✨ Features

* 🎯 **Activity-Specific Risk Analysis** — Walking, running, cycling, hiking, sports, travel, events & more
* 🌡️ **Multi-Factor Risk Engine** — Heat, rain, wind, UV, visibility & exposure duration
* 🧠 **Explainable Recommendations** — Understand *why* a risk score was generated
* 🕐 **Optimal Time Detection** — Finds safer/better windows within the forecast
* 📈 **Forecast Risk Progression** — Track how risk changes throughout an activity
* 🌍 **Live Weather Data** — Powered by Open-Meteo
* 🛡️ **Production Reliability** — Timeouts, retries, caching and request cancellation

## 🧠 How It Works

```text
Weather Data
     ↓
Environmental Conditions
     ↓
Activity Context
     ↓
Exposure Duration
     ↓
Risk Engine
     ↓
Risk + Explanation + Best Time
```

> **Weather tells you what is coming.
> Sentinel tells you what it means.**
